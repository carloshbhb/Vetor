# Learjet 60 documentary pilot — editorial notes

- Event: Learjet 60 crash near Greenacres, Florida, November 1, 2026.
- Operator: Aurora Aviation, doing business as Upper Jet.
- Route: Austin-Bergstrom International Airport (Texas) to North Eleuthera Airport (Bahamas).
- Fatalities: nine people aboard.
- Crew communications referenced icing and turbulence; these are investigative context, not a proven cause.
- NTSB investigation ERA27MA026 remains ongoing. The cockpit voice recorder was recovered at the initial impact site and sent to the NTSB Vehicle Recorders Laboratory in Washington, D.C. (NTSB update, November 9, 2026).
- The route graphic is schematic, not a navigational map. Radar, recorder, and impact-area graphics are editorial illustrations, not recovered footage or actual cockpit/radar recordings.
- No crash impact reconstruction, victim imagery, or speculative causal conclusion is used.
