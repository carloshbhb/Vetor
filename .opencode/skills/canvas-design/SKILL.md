# Canvas Design

Create original visual designs through a two-step process:
1. Design Philosophy Creation (.md file)
2. Express on canvas (.pdf or .png file)

## Step 1: Design Philosophy

Create a VISUAL PHILOSOPHY that will be interpreted through:
- Form, space, color, composition
- Images, graphics, shapes, patterns
- Minimal text as visual accent

### Name the Movement

Give it a 1-2 word name: "Brutalist Joy" / "Chromatic Silence" / "Metabolist Dreams"

### Articulate the Philosophy

4-6 paragraphs covering:
- How the philosophy manifests through space and form
- Color and material choices
- Scale and rhythm
- Composition and balance
- Visual hierarchy

**Emphasize craftsmanship** - The final work should appear meticulously crafted, labored over with care, the product of deep expertise.

## Step 2: Canvas Creation

Express the philosophy on a canvas:
- Create one single page, highly visual, design-forward output
- Use repeating patterns and perfect shapes
- Sparse, clinical typography
- Limited color palette that feels intentional

**Text as contextual element** - Always minimal and visual-first.

## Refinement

- Don't add more graphics; refine what exists
- Make the composition more cohesive
- Ensure everything is crisp and pristine

## When to Use

- Creating posters or art pieces
- Designing visual identities
- Creating static visual content
- When user asks for "design" or "art"
